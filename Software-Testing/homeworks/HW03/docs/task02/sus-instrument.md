# SUS Questionnaire — EMS (Event Management System)

> **Instrument:** System Usability Scale (Brooke, 1996)  
> **When to administer:** After the participant completes ALL tasks — do NOT administer between tasks or before the task  
> **Time to complete:** ~2–3 minutes

## Instructions for Participant

_Read aloud to participant before handing them the questionnaire:_

> "I have a short questionnaire for you to fill in. For each of the following statements, please select the number that best describes your reaction to the system you just used. There are no right or wrong answers — your honest first impression is what matters. Use this scale: **1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree**."

## Scoring Formula (For Facilitator/Analyst Use Only)

**Step 1 — Adjust scores:**

- **Odd-numbered questions (1, 3, 5, 7, 9):** Adjusted score = Response − 1
- **Even-numbered questions (2, 4, 6, 8, 10):** Adjusted score = 5 − Response

**Step 2 — Compute SUS score:**

> **SUS Score = Sum of 10 adjusted scores × 2.5**

Range: **0** (worst) to **100** (best).

**Worked example:**

| Q       | Raw Response | Adjustment | Adjusted Score |
| ------- | ------------ | ---------- | -------------- |
| Q1      | 4            | 4 − 1      | 3              |
| Q2      | 2            | 5 − 2      | 3              |
| Q3      | 4            | 4 − 1      | 3              |
| Q4      | 1            | 5 − 1      | 4              |
| Q5      | 4            | 4 − 1      | 3              |
| Q6      | 2            | 5 − 2      | 3              |
| Q7      | 4            | 4 − 1      | 3              |
| Q8      | 1            | 5 − 1      | 4              |
| Q9      | 4            | 4 − 1      | 3              |
| Q10     | 1            | 5 − 1      | 4              |
| **Sum** |              |            | **33**         |

> SUS Score = 33 × 2.5 = **82.5**

## Interpretation Table (Sauro & Lewis, 2012)

| SUS Score Range | Grade | Adjective Rating      | Percentile (approx.) |
| --------------- | ----- | --------------------- | -------------------- |
| 90–100          | A+    | Excellent             | Top 10%              |
| 85–89           | A     | Excellent             | Top 15%              |
| 80–84           | B+    | Good                  | Top 25%              |
| 75–79           | B     | Good                  | Top 35%              |
| 70–74           | C+    | OK                    | Top 45%              |
| 68              | C     | OK (industry average) | 50th percentile      |
| 65–67           | C-    | OK                    | 40th percentile      |
| 60–64           | D     | Poor                  | ~30th percentile     |
| 51–59           | D-    | Poor                  | ~20th percentile     |
| ≤ 50            | F     | Awful                 | Bottom 15%           |

> **Industry average: 68** (Sauro, MeasuringU benchmark database, n > 10,000 studies). A score of **80.3** represents the **top quartile**.

## Administration Rules

- ✅ Administer SUS **after all tasks are complete**.
- ✅ Instruct participants to answer based on their **first impression**.
- ✅ Allow ~2–3 minutes; do not rush.
- ❌ Do NOT administer SUS before tasks or between tasks.
- ❌ Do NOT explain what individual statements mean — they are standardised and must not be paraphrased.
- ❌ Do NOT show the scoring formula to participants.
